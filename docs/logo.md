## Generation prompt

> Design a single distinctive square app icon for Video Producer, a professional editing tool. On a solid deep midnight-plum rounded-square tile, form a right-facing play arrow from three chunky offset strips of luminous apricot and coral film. Cut crisp diagonal negative-space seams between the strips, like three adjacent clips meeting at an edit. Bold flat geometric silhouette, optical centering, very generous clear margins, subtle tactile print finish, immediately legible at 32 pixels. Transparent canvas outside the tile. No lettering, camera, mockup or extra symbols.
