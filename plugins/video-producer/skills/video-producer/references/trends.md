# Reels radar

1. Start with the project's watchlist.local.json if provided, then use bundled watchlist.json for starter sources. Search recent posts and pinned metadata, using the audience and topic from the brief when the watchlist has no relevant handles. Record collection timestamp, publication date, platform and visible metrics. Use open sources first, then vidIQ profile Reels and Instagram outlier search if connected and within the authorized quota. Keep Instagram, YouTube Shorts and long-form performance separate.
2. For breakout claims, compare a Reel's public plays to the median of at least five age- and format-comparable recent Reels from its own creator if data is available. Show sample size and age and keep repeated uploads out of the sample. Label posts with incomplete data as candidates. Show cross-creator evidence before calling a format a trend. Identify country/audience and whether the clue is currently relevant for Uzbek viewers. Do not infer completion rate from views or likes.
3. Read Meta/Instagram trend and creator tools where accessible, including Reels trending audio where available. Treat audio popularity as a separate signal. Check permitted platform use of music and find an appropriate alternative for cross-posting if needed. Owned reach, retention and audience data require the owner's connected account.
4. Convert three to five examples into original concepts. Include the exact opening line and first visual, practical value, supporting demonstration or footage, source URL, intended length and shot complexity. Prefer a proof clip or demonstration that can be captured from the user's own product. Save selected links to the current project's `references/` when production begins.
5. Compare with previous days or weeks if records are available. When a scheduled radar already supplies a brief and sources, register its selected links in the local project and reuse the evidence.

Sources: [Meta Reels trends and insights](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/04/instagram-reels-trending-audio-and-gifts-updates/), [Meta Best Practices](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/), [vidIQ Instagram](https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/14975681-vidiq-for-instagram), [vidIQ MCP](https://support.vidiq.com/en/articles/15082430-vidiq-mcp).
